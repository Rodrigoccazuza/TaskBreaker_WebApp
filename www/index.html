    How importing works

    Task Breaker runs entirely in your browser with no server, so it can't do live two-way sync with Notion or Google (those need secure logins a static page can't hold). Instead, import your data any of these ways — it stays on your device, and Pathfinder can then organize it into your categories.

    Upload a file

    CSV or JSON. From Google Sheets: File → Download → CSV. From Notion: ••• → Export → CSV.

    Paste text

    One item per line. Add a date like 2026-10-15 or due: Oct 15 anywhere in the line.

    Published Google Sheet

    In Google Sheets: File → Share → Publish to web (CSV), then paste the link here. Or paste any public CSV link.

        About Task Breaker

        Task Breaker runs on four core principles:

        • Reduce the learning curve — everything in one tap or two.
        • Break down the task — no goal is too big once it's small pieces.
        • Have a sense of direction and progress — wheels, streaks and XP always show where you stand.
        • Have fun in the process — medals, celebrations and lock-in sessions keep it a game.

        Privacy & Data

        What Task Breaker keeps: your goals and tasks, notes, wishlist, routines, XP, medals and settings. That's it — the app only stores what you type into it.

        Where it lives: on this device, in your browser's local storage. When you sign in, a copy syncs to your own private cloud account (Supabase) so your other devices can pick it up. Nobody else can read it: every row is locked to your account.

        What it's for: running the app and improving your experience — your progress wheels, streaks, digests and suggestions. Pathfinder's suggestions are computed on your device; your tasks never leave it for analysis.

        What it never does: no ads, no trackers, no selling or sharing your data. Group goals and shared protocols will only ever use the same account sync, with your consent.
